# ExtraHands 👵🤝

**Trusted companionship for ageing parents, whenever families need an extra hand.**

ExtraHands is an on-demand elderly companion care platform designed to help families arrange reliable companionship for ageing parents at home. It connects families with companions for short-term or planned visits, making it easier to coordinate care while providing visibility into bookings and visit updates.

🔗 **Live Prototype:** https://ayanvsingh.github.io/extrahands-elderly-care/

## 🌟 Features

### 👨‍👩‍👧 For Families
- **Elder Profiles:** Maintain elder information, medical notes, mobility needs, and personal preferences.
- **Companion Discovery:** Browse companion profiles, ratings, reviews, hourly rates, languages, and verification status.
- **Flexible Booking:** Select the elder, date, start time, and visit duration.
- **Booking Management:** View booking requests, confirmations, and visit statuses.
- **Visit Tracking:** Access check-in and check-out records, photos, and companion updates.
- **Family Reassurance:** Keep track of visits and receive information about the elder's care.

### 🤝 For Companions
- **Companion Dashboard:** View and manage assigned visit requests.
- **Booking Actions:** Accept or decline booking requests.
- **Visit Check-in and Check-out:** Record visit progress and completion.
- **Visit Updates:** Add notes and photos to communicate relevant updates.
- **Identity Verification Workflow:** Upload identity documents and view their verification status in the prototype.

### 🎨 User Experience
- Responsive, mobile-oriented interface
- English, Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu language options
- Light and dark themes
- Local browser storage to retain prototype data
- Home-screen installation prompt for supported browsers

## 🎯 Problem Statement

In many Indian families, adult children balance work and personal commitments while coordinating care for ageing parents. Existing arrangements may not provide flexible, short-duration companionship when family members are unavailable.

ExtraHands explores an accessible way to discover companions, schedule visits, record care preferences, and keep family members informed.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

ExtraHands uses an on-demand marketplace model built around a network of verified companions. Families can store an elder's needs once, find a suitable companion, make a booking, and follow the visit through check-in records and updates.

The concept is designed for both planned care and situations where additional companionship is needed at short notice.


## 🧪 Exploring the Prototype

1. Open the application.
2. Switch between **For families** and **I'm a companion**.
3. Explore elder profiles and the companion booking flow.
4. Create and manage bookings using the available demo workflows.
5. Switch to the companion view to explore booking requests, identity verification, and visit tracking.
6. Try the language selector and light/dark theme controls.

Some workflows simulate real-world processes for demonstration purposes.
